# 1. About AJNETWORKS

AJNETWORKS is an enterprise technology consultancy headquartered in Nairobi, Kenya.

We help organizations design, build, secure and optimize technology systems that enable sustainable business growth.

AJNETWORKS combines software engineering, cybersecurity, cloud technologies, networking, digital strategy and business process optimization into one integrated consultancy.

We do not position ourselves as freelancers, an outsourcing agency, or a low-cost managed service provider.

We position ourselves as trusted technology advisors and engineering partners.

# 5. Tone of Voice

Every page should communicate like a senior technology consultant.

Avoid buzzwords such as:

- innovative
- cutting-edge
- world-class
- revolutionary
- disruptive

Instead communicate outcomes.

Example:

Bad

"We provide innovative digital solutions."

Good

"We help organizations simplify operations through secure software engineering and modern infrastructure."
