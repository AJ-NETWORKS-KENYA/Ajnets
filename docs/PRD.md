# AJNETWORKS — Product Requirements Document (PRD)

> **Version:** 1.0.0  
> **Maintainer:** AJNETWORKS Product Team  
> **Target Domain:** https://ajnetworks.co  

---

## 1. Vision & Mission
AJNETWORKS delivers high-performance enterprise IT infrastructure, managed network solutions, cybersecurity, cloud integrations, and digital transformation consulting across East Africa (HQ in Kenya, operations in Rwanda).

## 2. Product Objectives
- Provide an authoritative, high-performance web experience representing AJNETWORKS services.
- Drive lead generation and consultation requests via seamless contact workflows.
- Showcase case studies, technical insights, and enterprise service offerings with clear information architecture.
- Maintain 100% brand integrity, fast loading speeds (<1.5s FCP), and SEO compliance across all pages.

## 3. Core Features
- **Corporate Service Pages:** Enterprise IT, Managed Networks, Cloud Infrastructure, Cybersecurity, Solutions Consulting.
- **Insights & Articles:** Thought leadership, industry analysis, technical case studies.
- **Company & Location Hubs:** Regional presence in Kenya and Rwanda with direct communication channels.
- **Lead Capture & Contact:** Direct inquiry forms, dynamic email handlers, and interactive communication links.

## 4. Success Metrics
- **Performance:** 95+ Mobile and Desktop Lighthouse Scores across all key pages.
- **Brand Consistency:** 100% test & evaluation compliance for links, social profiles, and JSON-LD schema.
- **Lead Flow:** Continuous intake of verified service inquiries.
